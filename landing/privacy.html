Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

What data we collect

When you sign up for an account, we collect your email address. When you use the API, we log the endpoint called, timestamp, and HTTP status code for usage tracking. We do not store the HTML content or PDF files you convert.

How we use your data

Your email is used for account management and to send payment receipts via Lemon Squeezy. Usage logs are used to enforce plan limits and to display your usage dashboard. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

API keys

API keys are stored hashed and are used to authenticate requests. You can regenerate your API key at any time from the dashboard.

Cookies

We use Supabase Auth for session management, which sets necessary cookies. No tracking or analytics cookies are used.

Contact

Email contact@uhadev.com with any privacy concerns.
